    Nutrition & Lifestyle Reference

    Personal notes + safety guardrails

    Blood pressure support

    Low carb meals, low sodium, steady weight loss if needed, sleep, stress control, cycling, resistance training, isometric work, yoga, slow breathing, and consistent medicine logging.

    Nutrient targets

    Potassium target: 4,700mg from food when appropriate. Magnesium target: 800mg from magnesium glycinate per your notes. Vitamin D, zinc, iodine, vitamin C, B1, fish/cod liver oil, garlic extract, L-arginine, celery, hibiscus, beetroot, cacao, and vitamin E are tracked as notes.

    Food scoring

    Helpful: avocado, eggs, meat, salad, spinach, swiss chard, beet tops, celery, hibiscus tea, beetroot, cacao, fish, low-carb vegetables. Caution: sugar, bread, rice, pasta, potatoes, starch, sweet drinks, high-salt processed foods.

    Daily limit calculation

    The app estimates food totals by matching logged foods to a serving database. It compares estimated potassium, magnesium, net carbs, glycemic quality, and carb-to-fiber ratio against your daily limits, then combines those estimates with any manual nutrient numbers you enter.

    Safety

    Do not stop prescribed medicine because of app feedback. Ask your clinician before high-dose supplements or potassium products, especially with kidney disease or medication changes.
